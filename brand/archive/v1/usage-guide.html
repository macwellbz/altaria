Logo guidelines

How to use the logo: which version to pick, how much room to give it, how small it can go, its colours, and what to avoid. Use the supplied files; never redraw or retype it.

The idea. A ticket whose tear is the horizon: the booked, safe half below, and the sun rising over the destination above.

02

Primary or secondary

Use ‘altaria ai’ (primary)

  • By default: the site header, app stores, first mention, print, slides.
  • Anywhere the reader might not know what the product is.

Use ‘altaria’ alone (secondary) when

  • Space is tight: narrower than the primary's 120 px (30 mm) minimum, or a short bar such as a mobile header.
  • The context already says it's an AI tool: inside the product after the first screen, or an AI directory where ‘AI’ is the category.
  • ‘ai’ would repeat: next to a headline, handle or tagline that already says AI.
  • Partner and press lists that show names without descriptors.

Use the symbol alone when

  • It's an icon: favicon, app icon, social avatar.
  • The name is already right there, e.g. a watermark beside a caption that says ‘Altaria AI’.
  • Never as a replacement for the name on first contact.
03

Construction

Units are the symbol file's own (204 across).
  • Ticket: a 204 × 204 square with a corner radius of 30.
  • Tear: a straight 12-unit cut, placed on the golden section, so the visible sky : stub = 118.66 : 73.34 = 1.618 : 1 (61.1% down).
  • Notches: radius 18, centred on the tear at both edges.
  • Sun: Dawn coral, radius 56, resting on the tear's top edge inside a 12-unit ring. The ring and the tear are the same width.
  • Lockups: the symbol is 1.15 × the height of the ‘l’, and the gap to the wordmark equals the sun's radius. ‘altaria’ is Commissioner at weight 540, ‘ai’ at 340, both with full flare (FLAR 100), outlined.
04

Clear space

Keep a margin of R on every side, where R is the sun's radius at the size you use.
